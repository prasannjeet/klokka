Klokka is time reporting for small businesses: the employer logs the hours each employee worked, day by day, and both sides see the same month add up. The app is free to use and open source.

FOR THE EMPLOYER
• Log hours per person and day, or a whole week at once.
• Split a day into jobs, with a start time, a place and a note.
• Round to the quarter or half hour and set a standard working day.
• Close the month when it is right and export it as CSV for payroll.
• Show pay if you want: each employee gets an hourly rate and sees the amount next to the hours.

FOR THE EMPLOYEE
• See the month's days, the total and the average per working day.
• Get a notification when hours are added or changed, one per change.
• Flag a line that looks wrong, with a message to the employer.
• Every change is kept in a history both sides can see.

ONE ACCOUNT, SEVERAL BUSINESSES
Work in two places? Switch between the businesses in the same app.

SWEDISH AND ENGLISH, LIGHT AND DARK
The app follows the phone's language and theme, and you can choose yourself.

Klokka is also a web app at app.klokka.se. The source code is MIT licensed on GitHub.
