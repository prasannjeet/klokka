For employers

Log a week in a minute. Then get on with the day.

Nothing to configure, nothing to learn. Open the week, type the hours, done. Klokka tells the people it concerns.

  • The week grid

    Every employee, every day of the week, one screen. Tab across it like a spreadsheet.

  • Quick chips

    0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8 and a full day. Plus Same as yesterday for the days that repeat.

  • A note when it matters

    Opened early, covered for Jonas, inventory night. One line, kept with the entry.

  • Close the month

    Lock it, export the CSV, hand it to whoever runs the pay. Unlock if something comes up.

  • Flags come to you

    An employee disagrees, you get a push. Fix it or dismiss it; both sides are told.

  • History on every entry

    Who changed what, and when. Visible to both of you.

For employees

Your hours, on your phone, the moment they change.

No asking, no photo of a paper sheet in the break room. The month is there with its total, and you are told every time it moves.

  • See the month

    Every day you worked, the total, the average per working day, and how it compares to last month.

  • Get told, once

    Hours added or changed? One notification per sitting, in the app and as a push.

  • Flag what looks wrong

    Worked 6 h, not 4? Say so on the entry. The employer gets it, you get the answer.

  • Share your month

    A card that says Your September: 142 h, made to save or send.

  • Two employers, one app

    Work at two places? Switch between workspaces; each keeps its own hours and its own colour.

  • Dark mode, obviously

    Follows your phone. Or pick one.

The pay switch

Hours only. Or hours and pay.

One switch in workspace settings. Off, Klokka is a clean record of hours and you do money elsewhere. On, every employee has an hourly rate and every hours figure gets a money figure beside it. The employee sees it too. That is the point.

Off

Hours only. Nobody sees a rate.

On

Rates per person. Money next to every hour.

Free and open source

Free is not a plan. It is the licence.

Klokka is MIT licensed. Use it, change it, run it for your own business or for others. The hosted instance we run is the same code, with no premium branch.

Questions

Before you ask.

Can employees log their own hours?

Not in the first version. The employer logs, the employee sees, and can flag an entry that looks wrong. A clock-in timer and self-logging are on the list, after the shared record is right.

Is it really free?

Yes. MIT licence, no tiers, no per-seat pricing. We run a hosted instance you can use, and you can run your own from the same code.

Do I have to show pay?

No. Pay is a workspace switch, off by default. Turn it on and every employee gets an hourly rate and sees money next to hours. Turn it off and it is hours only.

Which languages?

English and Swedish from day one, in both apps. The strings are shared between web and mobile, so the two never drift apart.

Which phones?

Android first, and the web app works in any phone browser. The code is iOS ready; the App Store build comes when the account does.

Where does my data live?

On our hosted instance in the EU, in a database that stores hours, not passwords. Sign-in is handled by Logto. Export or delete a workspace at any time.

What happens when a month is closed?

The month locks: no edits until the employer unlocks it. Employees are told, and the CSV is ready for whoever runs the pay.

What is a flag?

An employee's way to say an entry is wrong, with a message. The employer fixes or dismisses it; both sides are notified and the history shows what happened.
