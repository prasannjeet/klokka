# Jobs and recurrence, job notifications, digest with Excel: production release v1.7.0 (CHQ-185 to CHQ-190)

Released 2026-10-24 at the owner's request, after local, staging and test-phone checks.

## What changed

- CHQ-185: the job editor is a full page on the phone and the web; leaving with unsaved changes asks "Discard
  changes?". A day's plus adds a single job; New job has a date and a repeat.
- CHQ-186: monthly repeat on a day of the month or an nth weekday (third Wednesday, last Friday); repeats with no end
  date are planned 12 months ahead and topped up nightly (`RecurrenceExtender`).
- CHQ-187: editing a repeating job asks which jobs change (only this, this and following, all, past included; closed
  months are skipped and counted); a series' schedule can be changed from any of its jobs.
- CHQ-188: `JOB_CHANGED` notifications to the employee, coalesced per sitting, with a Job updates setting and an
  Android "Job updates" channel.
- CHQ-189: the weekly digest is on for everyone (V7 switched every existing user on), redesigned, with an Excel
  workbook per workspace (Jobs and Hours sheets, employer and employee versions, pay where shown). The mail quota is
  daily over a rolling 24 hours (`KLOKKA_MAIL_DAILY_QUOTA`, 100) with a digest reserve of 20; digests go out Monday
  07:00 to Wednesday (D22). The operator meter shows the last 24 hours.
- CHQ-190: the phone sends a tab of the other role to the signed-in role's first tab after an account switch.
- Schema: V6 (recurrence modes, open series, job updates preference) and V7 (digest on by default).

## Rollback

Previous production tag `v1.6.6`. V6 and V7 are additive (new columns, a default and a data update): v1.6.6 runs on
the V7 schema (every new NOT NULL column has a default, and Flyway ignores migrations newer than the code). Rolling back does not switch the digest off again for users V7 switched on.
