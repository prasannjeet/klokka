# Location search fix, production release v1.6.4 (CHQ-176)

Released 2026-10-07 at the owner's request, after the fix was checked on the test phone against staging.

## What changed

- The job location search keeps the tapped result in the search field, on the phone (Android and iOS share
  `LocationPicker.tsx`) and on the web ("Change" reopens the search with the picked text). Tapping the field again
  shows the results again, and typing on (a house number) searches the longer text. On the phone the search runs
  only while the field has focus, so the kept text costs no background Google call.
- Android: a picked place moves the map there instantly. Google cancelled the animated flight when the keyboard
  closed and the map resized (`GoogleMapsView.setCameraPosition` rejected, "Animation cancelled"); a camera
  resting short of the place could rename it after the wrong spot. expo-maps drops that promise, so it cannot be
  caught and retried. iOS (Apple Maps) is unchanged.
