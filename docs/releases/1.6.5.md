# Invitation for another account, production release v1.6.5 (CHQ-178)

Released 2026-10-10 at the owner's request, after a browser test on staging.

## What changed

- An invitation opened while signed in with another email no longer offers Join (which failed afterwards with
  `INVITATION_EMAIL_MISMATCH`). The web join page compares the session's email (ID token) with the invitation
  before it shows any action: it says who is signed in and who the invitation is for, and offers "Sign out to
  accept". Logto only returns to the base URL after sign-out, so the join page waits in a 10-minute cookie
  (`klokka_after_sign_out`) and the signed-out app shell sends the browser back to it, where the invited address
  signs in or registers. The phone's in-app invitation screen shows the same text and Sign out instead of Join.
- The day screen's recurring-job tests use a Wednesday a week ahead: the fixed 2026-10-07 had passed, and Repeat is
  offered only from today on (it failed the first staging CI of this change).

## Test

Staging, Playwright at 390x844, signed in as the staging employer, opening a pending invitation for another
address with `accept=1`: no Join button, the explanation shown, no accept request sent. "Sign out to accept" ended
the session (`/api/k/me` 401) and landed back on the same `/join?token=...`, signed out, with Join offered.
