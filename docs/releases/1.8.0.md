# Calendar jobs, job titles, phone person view, totals over 24 h: production release v1.8.0 (CHQ-191)

Released 2026-10-25 at the owner's request, after local, staging and test-phone checks.

## What changed

- **Totals over 24 h (customer bug).** A sum of several jobs went through the one-job duration formatter, which
  caps at 24 h, so an employee of STÄDMINATOR Golv och Puts (analysis off for employees) saw "24 h" for October on
  the web while the owner's phone showed 60.25 h. The employee month card, the employer's "so far, planned" line,
  the web team calendar's day total and the phone's team day and Home totals now use decimal hours (`formatHours`).
- **New job from a calendar date.** Web calendar (selected day) and phone team day: one eligible employee opens the
  single-job editor directly, several open a picker (web menu, phone sheet); an empty day offers Add a job; a closed
  month shows the button disabled with the reason. Save returns to the same day.
- **Job title.** Optional, at most 120 characters, the job's main label everywhere (place on the second line), in
  push texts, reminders, the digest email and a Title column in its workbook (D23). A `JobWrite` that leaves `title`
  out keeps the title, so 1.7.0 phones and stale web tabs cannot wipe titles.
- **Phone person screen** opens on Month; Back from a day opened from Month or Week returns there (button and
  gesture).
- Schema: V8 (`job.title`, `job_recurrence.title`, nullable `varchar(120)`).

## Rollback

Previous production tag `v1.7.0`. V8 is additive (two nullable columns) and v1.7.0 does not map them, so pinning
`v1.7.0` runs on the V8 schema. While rolled back, titles stay in the database but are not shown, and series jobs
planned by the 1.7.0 extender get no title.
