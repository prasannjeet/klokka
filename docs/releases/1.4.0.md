## Google Play public publishing remains blocked (CHQ-153)

Google rejected public promotion with `Precondition check failed`. Attempting a completed closed-testing release
then returned `Only releases with status draft may be created on draft app`. The API accepts internal testing
and a closed-testing draft, but the app has not completed its initial publishing setup/review.

Next in Play Console: complete the app-content and initial publishing steps, configure the closed-test testers,
and submit the prepared v1.4.0 draft for review. The launch runbook records the personal-account closed-test
requirement (12 testers opted in continuously for 14 days), then application for production access.
See [Play setup answers and commands](../PLAY_STORE.md) and [Google's testing requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465).

The first CI upload was interrupted by a concurrent status-check edit; rerunning the failed job succeeded.
The Play runbook now documents the one-edit-at-a-time rule and the correct promotion status flag. No active
Play upload/edit is left running.
