# Second job on the same day, production release v1.6.6 (CHQ-180)

Released 2026-10-14 at the owner's request, after a check on the test phone with the staging APK.

## What changed

- Phone: the plus next to a person on People always opens a new job for today, also when the day already has one or
  more; a tap on a day with jobs in the week views opens the Day page (each job its own card, plus Add another job);
  an empty day still opens the new-job sheet. Before, a day with exactly one job opened that job for editing, so
  "adding" a second job overwrote the first (`apps/mobile/src/features/jobs/dayAction.ts`, `PeopleScreen.tsx`).
- API, web and landing: no code change (the web day panel already offered Add another job); rebuilt at the new version.

## Test

Staging APK from `9900a1a` on the test phone, signed in as the staging employer: People plus on Lincoln, 1 h saved;
plus again opened "New job for Lincoln, day total 1 h so far"; 2 h saved, the day showed 2 jobs and 3 h; the day tap
opened the Day page with both cards and Add another job. The test jobs were removed afterwards.
