### 2.3 What the structures tell us [I]
1. **Head term "tidrapportering" is held by age + exact-match domains** (tidrapport.nu, tidrapport.app) and ekonomi
   brands (Spiris, Fortnox, Bjorn Lunden). A new domain will not break into that top 10 within months. Don't aim the
   homepage at it; aim it at "gratis tidrapportering / tidrapport för anställda / timlista app".
2. **"Free" is owned by one foreign player (Jibble)** with machine-translated programmatic pages. Its Swedish pages
   carry no Swedish specifics (arbetstidslagen, semesterersättning, timanställd). A Swedish, genuinely free, open-source
   product has a credible differentiation story and PR angle Jibble cannot copy.
3. **Every serious competitor runs three low-cost page types**: free template (mall), calculators, industry pages.
   Glossaries (Blikk 188, Ordio ~25 sv, Connecteam 262) and comparison/alternative pages (Clockify 23, Connecteam 72,
   Ordio 54) are the next tier.
4. **Question-shaped blog posts win informational SERPs** (Nuba, timebuddy-ai.se, humahr.com, flitz.ai). Young domains
   rank there.
5. **App stores take 4 of 9 slots on "tidrapport app"**. The Play Store listing is a ranking asset in itself.
